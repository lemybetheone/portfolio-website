01

Formula 1 analytics pipeline

Every season since 1950, pulled from a rate-limited public API, landed immutably in S3, loaded into Postgres, and modelled into a tested Kimball star schema with dbt. Orchestrated nightly by Airflow, served through Metabase.

dbtAirflowS3PostgresMetabase

Built solo

The part worth reading

One dbt test derives each championship total from the race and sprint facts and compares it against the official standings. It failed — 437 official points against 399 derived for 2024 — because sprint points were missing entirely.

Why the full history mattered

Backfilling every season since 1950 takes about seven and a half hours against a 500-request-per-hour budget. DNF rates fall from 49% in the 1950s to 13% in the 2020s, with a bump in the 1980s exactly where the turbo era belongs.

Decisions are written down

Sprint results are a separate fact table rather than a session_type column, because a fact whose grain needs a filter to be correct is a reliable source of wrong numbers. The alternatives that lost are logged in the repo.
